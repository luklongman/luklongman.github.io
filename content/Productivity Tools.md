## Digital note taking

If you're a MacOS user and just started journaling, I highly recommend Apple Notes (2018-) with [ProNotes by Dexter Leng](https://www.pronotes.app/) (2023-, free tier) to explore your habit and style. Having access to Markdown syntax, Bi-directional links and Global Search Palette in Notes is awesome.

Later I took part in projects that demanded deeper researches. That's when I occasionally used Notion and Obsidian, before fully switching to [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) (2026-). 

I also keep [[Handwritten Notebooks]] for bullet journalling and brainstorming.
