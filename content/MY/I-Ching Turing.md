[Turing Machine by Music Thing Modular](https://www.musicthing.co.uk/Turing-Machine/) is a popular Eurorack synth concept and sequencer module. The design is well documented in the official website.

The essence of the design lies on the concept of **bitwise rotation** - the binary status 

The I-Ching Turing Sequencer is made based on the original with the following spins.
- It uses 6 bits instead of 8, 2^6 = 64, instead of 256 variation
- bitwise


In VCV Rack, a clone is available. [Add Turing Machine by Stellare Modular](https://library.vcvrack.com/StellareModular/TuringMachine) to your library to try it out.
